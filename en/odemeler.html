Approval

Multi-signature by amount band

One, two or three signatures are required depending on the thresholds. The number needed is frozen at request time and cannot be lowered later by changing the threshold.

A requester cannot sign their own batch; the rule is enforced in the database. Changing the band table itself is also subject to approval.

Segregation of duties

The preparer cannot approve

Even if the user who built the batch is on the signature list, they cannot approve their own batch. The rule cannot be bypassed from the application layer.

Frozen signature count

The count is fixed at request time

The band table in force when the batch was opened applies. Whether the threshold is later raised or lowered, that batch keeps its signature count.

Configuration error

If there are too few authorised signatories

The batch opens but cannot be approved. It does not sit silently in the queue; it is reported as a configuration error.

Delivery

Two routes: file or direct delivery

File

ISO 20022 pain.001 and bank-format files are generated; you upload them to your bank. The download is recorded — an approved batch that was never downloaded lands on the attention list.

pain.001Bank-specific fileDownload record

Direct delivery

An approved instruction is delivered to the bank through a licensed open banking provider. Tideon holds no payment initiation licence; it produces the instruction and hands it to the provider.

Which route is used is decided per bank and per organisation.

Response tracking

Status per instruction

The bank response is processed per instruction. If one IBAN in a batch is wrong, only that instruction is rejected and the rest go through.

Accepted and received are not the same — the bank may have received an instruction without deciding on it yet. The two are shown separately.

What we do not do

We do not hold a payment initiation licenceInstructions are delivered through a licensed provider.
An approved batch cannot be editedA change produces a new batch; it can be cancelled but never deleted.
No total is given in mixed currenciesIf any line cannot be converted, no total is shown and approval falls to the highest band.
System approval is not bank approvalWhoever approves in the system may not be on the bank’s signature circular; the two authorities are kept separate.
