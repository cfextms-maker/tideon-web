Pricing

No complicated packages

One platform, every capability. Price is set by three things: how many entities, how many users, how many bank accounts. You never pay for modules you don’t use, because there are no modules.

Three dimensions

What sets the price

Number of legal entitiesYour holding company and its subsidiaries. Each entity is defined with its own chart of accounts, rate policy and permissions.
Number of usersAll roles included — treasury, accounting, audit, management. No extra charge per role.
Number of connected bank accountsThe number of accounts connected. What counts is how many accounts are read, not how many banks you work with.
