Why it matters

A risk you do not measure is not a risk you do not have

If a covenant cannot be tested, Tideon does not say “compliant” — it says “could not be tested”. An untested covenant is riskier than a breached one, because it means nobody is watching.

The same principle holds for every figure: a value that cannot be calculated stays empty and says why.
