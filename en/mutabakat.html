The bridge closes itself every morning: the gap must equal the total of unreconciled items. If it does not, data is missing, and it tells you so.

01

The system closes the easy part; the decisions are yours

Entries whose amount and date match are reconciled automatically. A low-confidence match stays a suggestion — so your time goes to the items that genuinely need a look.

02

An FX difference never misleads you

On a foreign-currency account, most of the gap comes from the exchange rate. The FX difference is calculated separately, so you see clearly how much of the gap is a real problem.

03

It is clear who stands behind every figure

The method, confidence and approver of every match stay on record. When an audit comes, you never have to search backwards.

On the accounting side

See the same balance sheet as your accountant

Chart of accounts codes are mapped to financial statement lines, and the mapping is tested against a real trial balance file. The difference between the balance sheet we produce and the one your accountant produces must be zero — until it is, we do not start calculating ratios.
