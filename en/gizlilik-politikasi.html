What data we collect

We collect the information you share when you fill in the contact form, and the technical data generated automatically when you visit the site. You never need to share anything the form does not ask for.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, to run the demo process, and to improve the site by understanding which parts are used.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data or pass it to third parties for marketing. Only the infrastructure providers we use (hosting, form handling, analytics) have technical access to it, and they are bound by contract as data processors.

Security

Site traffic runs over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to your data is limited to authorised staff.

How long we keep it

Contact requests are retained for 1 year. At the end of that period they are deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data and to request its correction or deletion. You can send your requests to info@tideon.com.tr.

Changes

If we change this policy, the current text is published on this page.
