About us

Tideon is a platform built to run the treasury function of multi-entity groups in Türkiye. It was written knowing exactly where spreadsheet-driven cash management fails: missing data, late information, and figures whose source is unclear.

Why

Why a CFO needs Tideon

A group’s cash position usually lives across several files and several people. It stays unclear which accounts the morning’s figure includes, or on what assumption it was calculated. The error shows up in large amounts, and late.

Tideon was written to close that gap. Every figure carries where it came from, which accounts it includes, and what data is missing. Unreadable data is never treated as zero; the account is excluded from the total, and the reason is written down.
